Hi everyone! Ever since I learned about protocols, I’ve started noticing them everywhere. From HTTPS securing my online shopping, to TCP ensuring smooth YouTube streaming, from SMTP and IMAP handling my emails, to SSH letting developers access remote servers safely. Even games use UDP for real-time communication, and VPNs rely on IPsec to protect our data. Give this article a read to explore how these protocols silently power our digital world.
