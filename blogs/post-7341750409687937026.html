India has taken a major step forward in quantum communication.

Ashmita Pandey · Jun 20, 2025 · Networking · Quantum Computing

India has taken a major step forward in quantum communication. DRDO and IIT Delhi have successfully demonstrated secure quantum communication over free space without using any fiber optic cables. They achieved over 1 km of secure data transfer using entangled photons through open air. Quantum Key Distribution was used to exchange secret keys securely. The system even detects if anyone tries to eavesdrop. The error rate remained below 7 percent. This is especially useful for defense communication in border areas where laying cables is not feasible. It’s more than just a security upgrade. It’s a shift in how we think about cybersecurity, satellite communication, and intelligence sharing. A great and potentially impactful breakthrough.
