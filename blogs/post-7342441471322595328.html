Hi everyone! Deadlocks are a critical concept in operating systems, often leading to system inefficiency or complete standstill when not handled properly. In this article, I have explained Deadlocks in Operating Systems in detail, including how they occur, the conditions required for them to exist, and various methods for prevention, avoidance, detection, and recovery. Real-world analogies have also been included to make the topic easier to understand. I invite you to read the article and share your insights. #OperatingSystems #DeadlockHandling
