Now I’ve started to understand the beauty of a curious mind.

Ashmita Pandey · Jul 1, 2025 · Career · Engineering Practices

Now I’ve started to understand the beauty of a curious mind. I feel like I’m in that phase where I want to explore everything. Startups fascinate me. I dream of building my own business empire. Development is something I enjoy and feel confident in. And over the years, I’ve been part of so many events, growth initiatives, and communities that shaped how I think. People often say we should focus on one thing. But maybe this is the phase to try more, learn faster, and let curiosity guide the way. Not everything needs to be figured out early. Sometimes, the best clarity comes from experience and not just planning.
