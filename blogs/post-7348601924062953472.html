Business logics and brainstorming the edge cases,

Ashmita Pandey · Jul 9, 2025 · System Design · Engineering Practices

Business logics and brainstorming the edge cases, asking what really matters, iterating again and again not just to make it work, but to make it right, fascinates me more than just writing code. It's about digging deeper, understanding the why behind every decision. These structures stay performant as the data grows. None of these decisions came from following tutorials. They came from building, breaking things, fixing them, and going through that cycle over and over. And with every iteration, the decisions became more instinctive. That’s the part of building I love most.
