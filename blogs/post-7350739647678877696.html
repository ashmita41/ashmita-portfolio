𝗛𝗼𝘄 𝗔𝗿𝗲 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗽𝘂𝘁𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗴𝗿𝗮𝗺𝗺𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗔𝗰𝘁𝘂𝗮𝗹𝗹𝘆 𝗥𝗲𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱? Think of a computer as a machine, like a car engine. On its own, it just sits there. To make it do something, you need to give it instructions. That’s where programming comes in. Programming is like writing a recipe that the computer follows step by step. Whether it’s:  • Showing a website  • Running a mobile app  • Calculating your expenses Every task happens because someone wrote code to tell the computer how to do it. A simple real-world example: When you click ‘Pay Now’ on a shopping site, the computer doesn’t automatically know what to do. A programmer wrote the logic behind what happens next i.e. verifying payment, sending confirmation, updating inventory, and more. At the core: Computer = Machine Programming = Instructions for the Machine It’s as simple and as powerful as that. If you're starting out, remember: Computers aren’t “smart” until you tell them what to do.
