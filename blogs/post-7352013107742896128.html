Just tried OpenAI’s ChatGPT Agent Mode and honestly… I’m amazed!

Ashmita Pandey · Jul 18, 2025 · AI · Agents

Just tried OpenAI’s ChatGPT Agent Mode and honestly… I’m amazed! You can now ask it to actually do things, not just answer questions or generate content. You can:  • Create a full competitor analysis report by scanning multiple websites and get it as a ready-to-share presentation.  • Plan a complete team trip, including flights, hotels, and activities, all organized neatly in a spreadsheet.  • Find potential hires from LinkedIn, shortlist profiles, and compile details into a document without manual searching.  • Draft follow-up emails or client messages and schedule them directly from your calendar events.  • Summarize unread emails and upcoming meetings every morning to help you start your day prepared. The fact that all of this runs in the background while you continue working… that’s next level. The idea of being a one-person team feels more realistic now. Have you tried Agent Mode yet?
