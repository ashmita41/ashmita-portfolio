OpenAI has released GPT-5, and it changes the way ChatGPT works!

Ashmita Pandey · Aug 8, 2025 · AI · LLMs

OpenAI has released GPT-5, and it changes the way ChatGPT works! The new model can automatically switch between quick responses and deep reasoning depending on the question, removing the need to choose models manually. With GPT-5, it is now possible to build and debug complete applications, get well-researched summaries, write context-aware content, analyze data, generate reports and visuals, plan projects, and connect with tools like Gmail or Calendar. It is significantly more accurate than previous versions, especially for coding and reasoning, and is available to all ChatGPT users, including those on the free plan. This is an exciting development. Do share findings and experiences with me!
