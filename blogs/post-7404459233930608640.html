𝗚𝗣𝗨𝘀 𝗰𝗵𝗮𝗻𝗴𝗲𝗱 𝗔𝗜 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝘆 𝗮𝗹𝗴𝗼𝗿𝗶𝘁𝗵𝗺 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗱𝗶𝗱! Neural networks existed in the 1980s. Backpropagation existed. Even large datasets existed. Yet AI only exploded recently. Why? Because CPUs think in sequence. GPUs think in parallel. And modern AI is nothing but: Millions of matrix calculations Happening at the same time. Originally, GPUs were built for one job: Rendering pixels in video games Today, they train models with Billions of parameters Same math. Different universe. Algorithms gave AI intelligence. GPUs gave AI speed. And speed is what unlocked scale. From: Training a single model in months To: Training thousands in days AI didn’t suddenly become smart. It finally became 𝗳𝗮𝘀𝘁 𝗲𝗻𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿.
