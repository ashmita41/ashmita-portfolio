I break down message queues from first principles, starting with a toy design and gradually layering in real-world constraints such as consumer failure, acknowledgements, retries, and concurrency. The goal is to understand why queues are designed the way they are, not just how to use them. If system design and distributed systems interest you, I’d love for you to give it a read and share your thoughts. Let’s connect and continue the conversation around building resilient systems.
