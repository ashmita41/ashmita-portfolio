In a monolith, transactions are easy.

Ashmita Pandey · Feb 23, 2026 · System Design · Microservices

In a monolith, transactions are easy. Everything runs inside a single database transaction. If one step fails, the entire operation rolls back. Microservices don’t work that way. Each service owns its own database, network calls can fail, and locking multiple databases in a single transaction isn’t practical. That’s where the Saga Design Pattern becomes useful. Check out my recent article that dives into the Saga pattern in microservice architecture. Do give it a read, and I’d love to discuss further!
