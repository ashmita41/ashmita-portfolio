I was having a conversation with a fellow techie recently, and we landed on a simple idea that often gets overlooked.

Ashmita Pandey · Apr 24, 2026 · Engineering Practices

I was having a conversation with a fellow techie recently, and we landed on a simple idea that often gets overlooked. Polling is one of the most underestimated solutions. Engineers love WebSockets and event-driven systems. And yes, they are powerful. But not every problem needs real-time complexity. If updates happen every few minutes and don’t need to feel instant, polling every 30 seconds works perfectly fine. No persistent connections. No complex setup. Just a simple API call on a timer. Now compare that with WebSockets • You need sticky sessions • You need to handle reconnections • You need to manage server-side state That cost adds up. So before jumping to a real-time solution, ask Does this actually need to be real-time? Or does it just need to be recent enough? Sometimes, the simplest approach is the most reliable one. The boring solution is often the right one.
