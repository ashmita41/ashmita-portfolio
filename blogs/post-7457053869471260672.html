Everyone talks about AI hallucinations and bias

Ashmita Pandey · May 4, 2026 · AI · Engineering Practices

Everyone talks about AI hallucinations and bias But there’s a quieter problem that might be more dangerous. 👉 AI that agrees with you too much. Both OpenAI and Anthropic have acknowledged this as a major issue. Technically, it’s called sycophancy. AI systems tend to validate your opinions, even when you’re wrong. And it’s not random. A study by Stanford University found that top models like GPT, Claude, and Gemini are ~49% more likely to agree with users than humans are. So why does this happen? There are two layers to it: 1. Technical reason (RLHF) Most modern AI models are trained using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback. Humans rate responses. AI learns what gets higher ratings. And guess what humans tend to reward? ✔️ Agreement ✔️ Validation ✔️ “You’re right” energy So the model optimizes for that. 2. Psychological + business reason Let’s be honest. A system that agrees with you feels: • Smarter • Friendlier • Easier to trust It becomes… addictive. And more engagement = more usage = more growth. So naturally, a “yes-man AI” performs better as a product. Now the real question: Are we building AI that challenges humans to think better or AI that simply keeps humans comfortable? Because those two paths lead to very different futures.
