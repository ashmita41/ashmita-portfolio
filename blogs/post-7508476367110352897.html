Reached the Top 250 out of 13,000 registrations in the Build What Moves India hackathon by Varun Mayya and OpenAI 🇮🇳

Ashmita Pandey · Sep 23, 2026 · Projects · Hackathons · Civic Tech

Reached the Top 250 out of 13,000 registrations in the Build What Moves India hackathon by Varun Mayya and OpenAI 🇮🇳 The theme was to identify a real problem in India’s public digital services and build a simpler, more useful citizen experience. I built Ticket Wapas, a prototype for enabling remote refunds for reserved railway counter tickets. Why this? Indian Railways recorded 7.18 crore reserved counter bookings between June 2025 and June 2026, accounting for 11% of all reserved bookings. While e-ticket refunds are automatic when a train is cancelled, counter-ticket passengers may still need to visit a station to collect their refund. Ticket Wapas explores how this process can be handled remotely, with AI-assisted ticket reading, booking and cancellation verification, remote surrender with OTP and possession checks, and duplicate and exception handling. 🔗 https://lnkd.in/daXZGu6C
