Had a great time speaking at the devSemble meetup on “Inside an AI Agent: Build It. Connect It. Secure It.”

Ashmita Pandey · Oct 10, 2026 · Speaking · AI Agents · MCP

Had a great time speaking at the devSemble meetup on “Inside an AI Agent: Build It. Connect It. Secure It.” We explored AI agents through a simple question: what does it take to build an agent that can actually do things, instead of just answering questions? From building our first agent with live demos to exploring MCP, security risks, human approvals, and memory, we covered quite a bit during the session. One of my favourite parts was breaking down these concepts through practical examples and seeing how the different pieces fit together. Thank you, Mihir Panchal and Prerak Gala, for making this happen, Paytm for hosting us, Dishant Gandhi for sharing the stage with me, and devSemble for bringing the community together. Really enjoyed the experience and the conversations around building AI agents! #AIAgents #MCP #AI #DeveloperCommunity
