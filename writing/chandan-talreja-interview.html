A Conversation with Mr. Chandan Talreja, Principal of Mahatma Gandhi English Medium School

  • Education
  • Interviews
Notes from a Studybase interview, not a transcript.

In this Studybase interview, school principal Chandan Talreja reflects on how moving from English-medium to Hindi-medium education as a child influenced his advocacy for affordable English-medium schooling.

Access and technology

The conversation looks at the Mahatma Gandhi School initiative, limited classroom infrastructure, and teachers’ use of digital tools during the pandemic. Talreja describes the confusion that can arise when schools use multiple apps and argues for better coordination with government education programs.
