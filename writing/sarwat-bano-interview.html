A Conversation with Ms. Sarwat Bano, Principal of Mahatma Gandhi English Medium School, Jaipur

  • Education
  • Interviews
Notes from a Studybase interview, not a transcript.

In this Studybase Media interview, Sarwat Bano, principal of Mahatma Gandhi English Medium School in Jaipur, describes how her experience teaching across Rajasthan shaped her approach to widening access and supporting students.

Three themes from the conversation

Bano discusses visiting families to understand attendance barriers, inviting girls to try school leadership for a day, and working with community partners to provide basic supplies. She also describes training teachers for online lessons during the pandemic and using virtual reality and robotics in the classroom.
